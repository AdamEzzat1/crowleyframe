## Queries

- `select`: select two columns
- `filter_mutate`: filter, then mutate two columns
- `group_mean`: group_by (100 groups), then mean
- `arrange`: arrange by two columns
- `join`: left join to a 100,000-row table
- `pipeline`: filter, mutate, group_by, summarise, arrange (5 verbs)
- `distinct`: distinct combinations of two columns
- `count`: count rows per group
- `add_count`: add a per-group row count to every row
- `transmute`: compute two columns, drop the rest
- `relocate`: move one column to the front
- `slice_min`: 10 smallest rows, keeping ties (raw: bottom_k, no tie handling)
- `slice_max`: 10 largest rows, no ties (raw: top_k)
- `drop_na`: drop rows missing either of two columns
- `replace_na`: replace missing values in two columns
- `fill`: fill missing values downward in one column
- `pivot_longer`: two value columns to long form
- `semi_join`: keep rows whose id is in a ~50k-row table
- `anti_join`: keep rows whose id is not in a ~50k-row table
- `across_mutate`: centre two columns with across() in mutate
- `across_summarise`: mean and sd of two columns per group with across()
- `long_pipeline`: drop_na, filter, mutate (if_else), replace_na, left_join, group_by, summarise (across), mutate, filter, arrange
